Key Findings

r = −0.71 between log(density) and green cover — the strongest relationship in the dataset. It's not just area: it's how many people are packed in.

93% of wards under 500 ha fail WHO's 9 m² standard. Below ~600 ha, there is almost no room for green. These are Vietnam's most concrete neighborhoods.

The mixed wards (phường + xã merges) cluster top-right — large and green — because they absorbed rural land. The pure urban wards tell the real density story.

Among pure-urban wards, density is the dividing line: above ~10,000 people/km², green cover collapses below 20%. That's the threshold where concrete wins.
